Local Setup Reference & Instructions

Quick commands for running Ollama and Tesseract on Windows, macOS, or Linux.

1. Running Ollama & Downloading Qwen2.5 3B

Download Ollama from ollama.com. Once installed, start the service and pull the 3B model:

ollama pull qwen2.5:3b

Qwen2.5 3B occupies only ~1.9 GB of RAM/VRAM and runs smoothly on standard modern laptops.

2. Tesseract OCR on Windows

If Tesseract is not installed on Windows, download the official Windows installer from UB-Mannheim Tesseract Wiki and ensure the installation folder (typically C:\Program Files\Tesseract-OCR) is in your system PATH.

3. 100% Privacy Guarantee

FreelanceFlow AI operates entirely on your workstation. No receipt images, merchant details, or financial figures are ever transmitted across the internet. SQLite stores your records in the local instance/ directory.
